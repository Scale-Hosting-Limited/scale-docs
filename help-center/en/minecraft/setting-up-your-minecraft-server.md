Once your Minecraft plan is active, your server is created automatically and you
can manage it from the game panel.

## First start

1. Open your service from **Services** and click through to the game panel.
2. Choose your **server type** — Vanilla, Paper, Spigot, Forge, or Fabric — and
   the **version** you want. Paper is a good default for most communities: it is
   fast and supports plugins.
3. Press **Start**. The first start downloads the server files and generates the
   world, so give it a minute.
4. When the console shows `Done`, your server is online.

## Connect

Your server address is shown on the panel (for example `play.yourname.scale…`).
In Minecraft, go to **Multiplayer → Add Server**, paste the address, and join.

## Accept the EULA

Minecraft requires you to accept Mojang's EULA. The panel prompts you on first
start; accept it and the server will boot. Nothing you host may break Mojang's
terms.
