Most account takeovers start with a convincing fake message, not a technical hack.
Knowing the signs protects you better than any setting.

## What we will never do

- We will **never ask for your password** — not by email, not in a ticket, not in
  Discord. Anyone who does is not us.
- We won't ask for your **2FA codes** or **recovery codes**.
- We won't DM you first on Discord asking you to "verify" your account by logging in
  through a link.
- We won't demand urgent payment in gift cards, crypto, or to a random personal
  account.

## Common scams

- **Fake "your server will be deleted" emails** with a login link — designed to
  panic you into entering your password on a lookalike page.
- **Fake staff in Discord** offering "support" via DM and sending a login or
  "verification" link.
- **Free upgrade / giveaway** links that ask you to "sign in to claim."
- **Fake invoices** for services you don't have.

## How to check a message is real

- **Look at the sender address**, not just the display name. Lookalike domains
  (extra letters, `.net` vs the real one) are the giveaway.
- **Hover the link** before clicking — does it go to our real domain? Better yet,
  don't click it: **type our address yourself** or use your bookmark, and check the
  panel directly.
- **We put notices in the panel too** — if an email says something urgent, sign in
  the normal way and confirm it's really there.
- If in doubt, **open a ticket** and ask before acting.

## If you clicked / entered details

1. **Change your password** immediately (see *Changing your password*).
2. **Sign out all sessions** and check for unfamiliar ones.
3. **Enable 2FA / a passkey** if it wasn't on.
4. Check your **account email** hasn't been changed.
5. Open a ticket so we can review the account.

## Protect yourself in advance

- Turn on **2FA or a passkey** — even if a scammer gets your password, they can't
  get in.
- Use a **unique password** so a leak elsewhere doesn't reach us.
- Be sceptical of urgency — "act now or lose your server" is a manipulation tactic,
  not how we operate.
