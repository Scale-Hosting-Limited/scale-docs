Your account can be signed in on more than one device at once — your laptop, your
phone, a shared computer. The security area lets you see and control those
sessions.

## View your sessions

Under **Account → Security**, look for **active sessions** (or devices). Each entry
typically shows the device/browser, rough location, and last activity. Your current
session is marked.

## Sign out a session

If you see something you don't recognise, or you signed in somewhere and forgot to
sign out (a friend's PC, a library), **revoke** that session. It's signed out
immediately and needs your credentials to get back in.

You can also **sign out everywhere** — useful if you think your account may be
compromised. You'll stay signed in only where you re-authenticate.

## When to review sessions

- After using a **shared or public computer**.
- If you get a **security alert** about a new sign-in you didn't make.
- Periodically, as good hygiene.
- Immediately if you suspect your password leaked — then also change it (see
  *Changing your password*).

## If you see an unfamiliar session

1. **Revoke it** (or sign out everywhere).
2. **Change your password** right away.
3. **Turn on 2FA / a passkey** if it isn't already (see *Enabling two-factor
   authentication*) — this stops a stolen password being enough on its own.
4. Check your **account email** for security and that it hasn't been changed.
5. If anything looks tampered with, open a ticket so we can help secure the
   account.

## Staying signed in

"Remember me" keeps a longer session on a device you trust. Don't use it on shared
computers, and revoke the session when you're done there.

## Troubleshooting

- **Signed out unexpectedly everywhere:** a password change or "sign out
  everywhere" was triggered — sign back in.
- **A session won't go away:** revoke it again and change your password; if it
  persists, contact support.
