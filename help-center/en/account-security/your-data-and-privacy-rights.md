We take your privacy seriously and give you real control over your data. This is a
plain-English summary; the **Privacy Policy is the authoritative document**.

## What we hold

- **Account data** — your name (for adults), email, and billing details.
- **Service data** — your servers, sites, and mailboxes, and what you store in
  them.
- **Operational data** — invoices, support tickets, and security/audit logs.

We don't store your full card number — payments are handled by our payment
providers. We don't sell your data.

## Your rights (UK/EU GDPR)

You can ask us to:

- **Access** — get a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Port** — receive that data in a portable format.
- **Rectify** — correct anything inaccurate.
- **Erase** — delete your data ("right to be forgotten"), subject to records we're
  legally required to keep (like invoices for tax).
- **Restrict / object** — limit or object to certain processing.
- **Withdraw consent** — for anything based on consent, such as cookies or
  marketing.

Most of these are self-service in the panel under **Account → Privacy** (data
export and erasure requests). You can also open a ticket.

## Cookies and marketing

- We ask for consent before non-essential cookies, and we honour "Global Privacy
  Control" browser signals.
- Marketing email is opt-in — you'll only get it if you asked for it, and you can
  unsubscribe any time.

## Exporting or deleting your data

- **Export:** request a data export from the privacy area; you'll get a copy of
  your personal data.
- **Delete:** request erasure the same way. Note that deleting your account also
  ends your services, so **download anything you want to keep first** (worlds,
  files, mailboxes). Some records (e.g. invoices) are retained where the law
  requires, then removed on schedule.

## Security is part of privacy

The best thing you can do to protect your data is secure your account — turn on
2FA or a passkey (see *Enabling two-factor authentication*), use a unique
password, and never share your login. If you think your account has been accessed
by someone else, change your password and contact us immediately.

## Questions

For any privacy question or to exercise a right that isn't self-service, contact us
via a support ticket. If you're ever unhappy with how we handle your data, you have
the right to complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).
