Two-factor authentication (2FA) means signing in needs your password **and** your
phone or a passkey. It's the best thing you can do to protect your services and
billing.

## Set up an authenticator app

1. Go to **Account → Security**.
2. Choose **Enable two-factor authentication**.
3. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, 1Password, etc.).
4. Enter the 6-digit code the app shows to confirm.
5. **Save your recovery codes** somewhere safe — they let you back in if you lose
   your phone.

## Prefer a passkey?

Scale also supports passkeys, which let you sign in with your device's fingerprint,
face, or PIN — no codes to type. Add one from the same **Security** page.

## If you get locked out

Use a recovery code to sign in, then re-set 2FA. If you've lost your recovery
codes too, open a ticket from an email address on the account and our team will
verify your identity and help you regain access.
