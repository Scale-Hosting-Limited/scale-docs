Change your password any time from **Account → Security**. It's worth doing
periodically, and immediately if you suspect anyone else has it.

## How to change it

1. Go to **Account → Security**.
2. Choose **Change password**.
3. Enter your **current** password, then your **new** one twice.
4. Save. You may be asked to confirm with 2FA.

For security, changing your password can sign out your other sessions — you'll
sign back in on those devices with the new password.

## Choosing a strong password

- **Long beats complex** — a passphrase of several random words is strong and
  memorable.
- **Unique to us** — never reuse a password from another site. If that site leaks,
  attackers try the same password everywhere.
- Use a **password manager** to generate and store a long random password; then you
  don't have to remember it.
- Avoid anything guessable (names, birthdays, "password123").

## After changing

- Turn on **2FA or a passkey** if you haven't — a password alone is the weakest
  setup (see *Enabling two-factor authentication*).
- Update the password anywhere you'd saved the old one.

## Forgot your password?

Use **"Forgot password"** on the sign-in page. We email a reset link to your
account address; follow it to set a new password. If you don't get the email,
check spam and confirm you're using the address the account is under.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Current password incorrect":** you may be signing in with a passkey/social
  login and not know the password — use "Forgot password" to set one.
- **Reset email didn't arrive:** wrong address, or it's in spam. Wait a few
  minutes; if still nothing, open a ticket from the account's email address.
- **Locked out entirely:** see *Recovering a locked account*.
