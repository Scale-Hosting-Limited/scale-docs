Your account email is where we send invoices, security notices, and password
resets, and it's often how you sign in — so keep it current and accessible.

## How to change it

1. Go to **Account** (profile/details).
2. Update the **email address**.
3. Confirm with your password or 2FA if prompted.
4. **Verify the new address** — we send a confirmation link to it. Click it to
   finish the change.

Until you verify, some features may stay tied to the old address, so complete the
verification step.

## Why it matters

- **Password resets and security alerts** go to this address — if it's an inbox
  you've lost access to, you could be locked out.
- **Invoices and renewal notices** go here too.
- If you sign in with this email, you'll use the new one afterward.

## Before you change it

- Make sure you can **receive mail** at the new address first.
- If you use **social login** tied to an email, keep them consistent so sign-in
  still matches.

## Didn't get the verification email?

- Check spam and wait a few minutes.
- Confirm you typed the address correctly.
- Some inboxes filter automated mail — try a different provider if it keeps
  failing, or open a ticket.

## Lost access to your old email?

If you can no longer receive mail at the old address and can't complete a change,
open a support ticket from any address and we'll verify you own the account before
updating it. We verify ownership precisely because this address controls account
recovery.

## Security tip

Use an email that itself has strong security (a unique password and 2FA). Your
account is only as secure as the inbox that can reset it.
