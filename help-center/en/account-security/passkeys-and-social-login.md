Besides a password, you can sign in with a **passkey** or a **linked social
account**. Both are quicker and, in the case of passkeys, more secure.

## Passkeys

A passkey lets you sign in with your device's fingerprint, face, or PIN — no
password to type or remember, and nothing phishable to steal.

**Add one** under **Account → Security → Passkeys**:

1. Click **Add passkey**.
2. Your device or browser prompts you to confirm with biometrics or your device
   PIN.
3. It's saved. Next time, choose "Sign in with a passkey."

You can add several (phone, laptop, a hardware key) so you're never locked out if
one device is unavailable. Give each a recognisable name.

## Social login (Discord)

If enabled, you can link your Discord account and sign in with it:

- Link it under **Account → Security** (or use "Sign in with Discord" and it
  matches your verified email/linked account).
- Linking is done from inside your signed-in account — that's what makes it safe.

Signing in this way still respects 2FA: if you have two-factor on, you'll still be
asked for it.

## Which should I use?

- **Passkeys** are the strongest and most convenient — use them if your devices
  support them.
- **Keep 2FA on** regardless (see *Enabling two-factor authentication*) as a
  backstop for password sign-in.
- Social login is a convenience; your account's own security (2FA/passkey) still
  matters.

## Managing and removing

Remove a passkey or unlink a social account any time under **Account → Security** —
for example if you lose a device or stop using a Discord account. Keep at least one
working sign-in method (and your recovery codes) so you don't lock yourself out.

## Troubleshooting

- **Passkey prompt doesn't appear:** your browser/device may not support them, or
  you're on an old browser — update it, or use password + 2FA.
- **Lost the device with your only passkey:** sign in with your password and 2FA
  (or a recovery code), then add a new passkey.
- **Discord sign-in says no account:** the Discord email/account isn't linked —
  sign in with your password first, then link it under Security.
