The Scale Discord bot lets you and your community see server status and run
approved actions from Discord.

## Invite the bot

1. From your service in the panel, open the **Discord** tab and click **Add to
   Discord** (or copy the invite link).
2. Choose the Discord server you want to add it to. You need **Manage Server**
   permission on that Discord to invite a bot.
3. Approve the permissions. The bot only requests what it needs to post status and
   respond to commands.

## Give it a channel

Pick (or create) a channel for the bot and make sure it can read and send
messages there. You can restrict the bot to a single channel if you prefer.
