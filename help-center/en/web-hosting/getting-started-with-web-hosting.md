Your web hosting gives you a control panel for files, databases, and email
routing.

## Find your control panel

Open your web hosting service from **Services** in the panel. From there you can
reach the file manager, databases, and your login details for SFTP/FTP.

## Point your domain

To use your own domain, update its DNS to point at your hosting:

1. In your domain registrar, set the **A record** to the IP address shown on your
   service (or use the nameservers we provide).
2. DNS changes can take a little time to propagate — usually minutes, sometimes up
   to a few hours.

If you bought your domain with us, this is set up for you.

## Add an SSL certificate

Enable HTTPS so visitors get a secure connection. Most plans include a free
certificate that is issued automatically once your domain points to the server.
