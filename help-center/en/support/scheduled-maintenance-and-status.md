Occasionally we need to perform maintenance, and very occasionally something breaks
unexpectedly. This explains how we keep you informed and where to look when you
suspect a platform-wide issue.

## Check status first

If a service seems down and you're not sure whether it's just you, **check our
status/maintenance notices** before opening a ticket. A known issue is usually posted
there with what's affected and an estimated fix — which saves you time and us a
duplicate ticket.

## Scheduled (planned) maintenance

Planned maintenance keeps the platform secure and reliable — updates, hardware work,
and improvements. When we schedule it:

- We **announce it in advance** where we can, with the date, time, and expected
  impact.
- We aim for **low-traffic windows** to minimise disruption.
- Most maintenance causes **little or no downtime**; when a restart is unavoidable,
  we tell you.

Keep your **account email** current so these notices reach you (see *Changing your
account email*), and add our address to your contacts.

## Unplanned incidents

Sometimes things break without warning. When they do:

- We **post an update** as soon as we've confirmed the issue.
- We **keep it updated** as we investigate and fix.
- We may follow up afterwards with what happened for significant incidents.

During an active incident, our team is focused on restoring service for everyone, so
individual ticket responses may be slower (see *Our response times and priorities*).

## What you can do during downtime

- **Check the status notice** for the latest and an ETA — refresh rather than opening
  repeat tickets.
- **Avoid making changes** to your service mid-incident; wait until it's stable.
- If your issue **isn't covered** by the posted incident, open a ticket so we know
  it's separate.

## Reducing the impact on you

- **Keep backups** so you can recover quickly from any disruption (see your
  product's backup guide).
- **Schedule your own restarts** away from typical maintenance windows.
- **Watch announcements** so planned work doesn't surprise you.

## After maintenance

Once work completes, your services should return automatically. If something doesn't
come back cleanly:

1. Try a **restart** from your panel.
2. Check the **console/logs** for errors (see *Reading server logs*).
3. If it's still wrong, open a ticket noting the recent maintenance.
