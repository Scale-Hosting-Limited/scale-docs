You can close your Scale Hosting account and have your personal data removed. This
explains how, what happens to your services and data, and the few things we're
legally required to keep.

## Before you start

Closing your account is significant and largely irreversible, so first:

1. **Back up anything you want to keep** — worlds, websites, mailboxes, databases.
   Once services are removed, that data is gone (see your product's backup guide).
2. **Cancel or let services lapse** — you can't keep a service on a closed account.
   See *Cancellations and refunds*.
3. **Move any domains** you want to keep to another registrar first (see
   *Transferring a domain away*) — otherwise they may lapse.
4. **Settle any outstanding invoices** so there's nothing left open.

## How to request deletion

Open a **support ticket** from the **email address on the account** (see *Opening a
support ticket*) and ask to close your account and delete your personal data. We'll
**verify you're the account owner** before proceeding — this protects you from
someone else closing your account.

## What gets deleted

Once verified and processed, we remove your **personal data** and your **service
data** (the content of your servers, sites, and mailboxes). Active services are
terminated as part of the closure.

## What we must keep — and why

Some records can't be deleted immediately because the law requires us to retain them:

- **Billing and invoice records** — kept for the period required by tax and
  accounting law.
- **Records needed to meet a legal obligation** or to resolve a dispute.

We keep the **minimum** necessary, for **only as long as required**, and delete it
after. We'll tell you what's retained and roughly for how long. This is the same
principle described in *Your privacy and data rights*.

## Timing

We action deletion requests within the timeframe the law requires (generally within
a month). Service data tied to terminated services may be removed sooner, on our
normal post-termination schedule.

## Changed your mind?

If you're closing because something went wrong, we'd genuinely like the chance to
help first — open a ticket and tell us what's not working. If it's about cost, ask
about downgrading (see *Upgrading or downgrading a service*). But if you've decided,
we'll respect it and process your request.

## Deleting data without closing the account

If you only want a **specific piece of personal data** removed rather than your whole
account, you can make an erasure request instead — see *Your privacy and data
rights*.
