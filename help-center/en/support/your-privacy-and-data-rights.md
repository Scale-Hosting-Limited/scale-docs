We take your privacy seriously and comply with data protection law, including the UK
and EU GDPR. This explains what we hold, the rights you have over it, and how to
exercise them. (For the account-holder view, see also *Your data and privacy
rights* in Account & Security.)

## What data we hold

To provide your services we process things like:

- **Account details** — name, email, contact and billing information.
- **Service data** — the servers, sites, and mailboxes you run, and their
  configuration.
- **Billing records** — invoices and payment references (we don't store full card
  numbers; payments are handled by our payment providers).
- **Technical logs** — sign-ins, security events, and system logs used to keep the
  platform secure and reliable.

We process this to deliver your services, meet legal and accounting obligations, and
keep the platform secure.

## Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to:

- **Access** — get a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Rectification** — correct anything inaccurate (much of this you can do yourself;
  see *Updating your profile and contact details*).
- **Erasure** — ask us to delete your personal data ("right to be forgotten"), where
  no legal obligation requires us to keep it.
- **Restriction / objection** — limit or object to certain processing.
- **Portability** — receive your data in a portable format.
- **Withdraw consent** — where processing relies on consent.

## How to make a request

Open a **support ticket** (see *Opening a support ticket*) from the email address on
your account, stating what you want (e.g. "data access request" or "erasure
request"). We'll **verify your identity** first — this protects you from someone
else requesting your data — then action it within the timeframe the law requires
(generally one month, occasionally extended for complex requests, and we'll tell you
if so).

## Deletion and legal retention

We honour erasure requests, but some records must be **kept for a period** to meet
legal and tax obligations (for example, invoices for accounting). We'll delete what
we can and explain anything we're required to retain and for how long. See
*Requesting account deletion* for the account-closure process.

## Data we share

We only share data with providers who help us run the service (such as payment
processors and infrastructure providers), under appropriate safeguards, and where
legally required. We don't sell your personal data.

## Cookies and tracking

Our sites use cookies needed to run the service and, where applicable, to understand
usage. You can control non-essential cookies through the cookie controls provided.

## Questions or concerns

If you have a privacy question or concern, open a ticket and ask for our data
protection contact. You also have the right to complain to your data protection
authority (in the UK, the ICO) — though we'd appreciate the chance to put things
right first.
