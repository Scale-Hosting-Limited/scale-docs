The fastest way to get help is a support ticket from your account — it reaches the
right team with your account already attached.

## How to open one

1. Sign in and go to **Support** (or **Tickets**).
2. Click **Open ticket**, choose the department (billing, technical, etc.), and
   pick the related service if asked.
3. Describe the issue and submit. We'll reply by email and in the panel.

## What to include (so we can fix it in one reply)

The more of this you give us up front, the faster it's resolved:

- **Which service** — the server address or the service name from your account.
- **What's wrong**, specifically — "server won't start" is a start; "crashes on
  boot after I installed a modpack, console shows a Fabric error" is far better.
- **What you changed** just before it broke.
- **The exact error** — copy the text or attach a screenshot. For game servers,
  the **last 20–30 console lines** (or `logs/latest.log`) are gold.
- **What you've already tried** from the relevant help article.

## What we can and can't do

- We can help with the platform, the panel, connectivity, and pointing you in the
  right direction on server software.
- We generally **don't** debug third-party plugins/mods or write your configs for
  you, but we'll help you read an error and isolate the cause.
- We will never ask for your password. Don't share it — we don't need it.

## Response times & urgency

We aim to answer as quickly as we can, and prioritise things that take a service
offline over questions and requests. Please keep one issue to one ticket and avoid
opening duplicates — it doesn't speed things up and it splits the history.

## Account security

Open tickets from the **account that owns the service**, or from an email address
on file. For sensitive actions we verify you own the account first — this protects
you from someone else requesting changes to your services.

## Emergencies

If a service is down or you suspect your account is compromised, say so clearly in
the subject line and change your password and check your 2FA straight away (see
*Enabling two-factor authentication*).
