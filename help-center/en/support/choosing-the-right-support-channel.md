We offer a few ways to get help, and each suits a different kind of question.
Picking the right one gets you a faster answer and keeps sensitive matters private.

## Support tickets — for anything account-specific

Open a **ticket** for anything tied to your account: billing, a service that isn't
working, verification, cancellations, abuse reports, or data requests. Tickets are
private, tracked, and reach our team directly. This is the **official** channel and
the right choice whenever your question involves your account details. See *Opening
a support ticket*.

## The Help Center — for how-to questions

Before opening a ticket, check here. This Help Center covers setup, configuration,
billing basics, security, and troubleshooting for every product. Most "how do I…"
questions are answered in minutes, any time of day.

## Community — for tips and general chat

Our community spaces (e.g. Discord) are great for general advice, sharing setups,
and chatting with other customers. Just remember:

- **Don't post private details** (invoices, personal info, server credentials) in
  public channels.
- **Official support happens in tickets**, not public chat — community helpers are
  friendly but can't access your account.
- **Beware impersonators.** Staff won't DM you first asking you to "verify" via a
  link (see *Recognising phishing and scams*).

## Status and maintenance — for "is it just me?"

If something platform-wide seems off, check our **status/maintenance notices**
before opening a ticket — a known issue may already be reported with an ETA. See
*Scheduled maintenance and status*.

## Which channel for what

| Your question | Best channel |
|---|---|
| Billing, invoices, refunds | Ticket |
| A service is broken | Help Center first, then ticket |
| Account recovery / verification | Ticket |
| Abuse report | Ticket (see *Reporting abuse*) |
| Data / privacy request | Ticket (see *Your privacy and data rights*) |
| "How do I…" | Help Center |
| General tips / chat | Community |
| Platform outage | Status page, then ticket |

## Stay safe wherever you get help

- We will **never** ask for your password or 2FA codes.
- Only trust support through **official channels** — the panel and our verified
  presences.
- Keep account-specific details in **private tickets**, never public posts.

Not sure? A ticket is always a safe default — see *Opening a support ticket*.
