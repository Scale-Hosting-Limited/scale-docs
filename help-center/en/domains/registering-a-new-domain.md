A domain name is your address on the internet — `yourname.com` instead of a string
of numbers. Registering one with us keeps everything in one place: your hosting,
your email, and your domain all under a single login.

## Search for a name

1. Go to **Domains → Register** in your panel.
2. Type the name you want and search. We'll show whether it's available and the
   price, plus suggestions across other endings (`.com`, `.co.uk`, `.net`, `.io`,
   and more).
3. Add the one you want to your cart. You can register several at once.

If your first choice is taken, a different ending (a **TLD**) is often available —
`.co`, `.dev`, or a country ending like `.uk` can work well.

## Complete the registration

1. In the cart, choose the **registration length** (one or more years). Longer
   registrations save you renewing as often.
2. Decide on **auto-renew** (recommended — it stops a domain lapsing by accident)
   and **domain privacy** if offered (see *Domain privacy and WHOIS*).
3. Pay for the order. The domain is usually registered within minutes.

## Registrant details

Every domain has a **registrant** — the legal owner — recorded with the registry.
We fill this from your account details, so make sure your name, address, and email
are accurate (see *Updating your profile and contact details*). Some endings need
extra info; we'll prompt you if so.

> **Verify your email.** ICANN rules require the registrant email on most domains
> to be confirmed. Watch for a verification email after registering — if you ignore
> it, the domain can be suspended.

## What you can do next

Once it's registered you can:

- **Point it at your hosting or server** — see *Pointing your domain*.
- **Set up DNS records** for websites, email, and more — see *Managing DNS
  records*.
- **Add email** on the domain — see *Setting up email DNS records*.

## Good to know

- **Registration isn't hosting.** A domain is just the name; you still need hosting
  or a server for it to point at. Many customers buy both together.
- **You own it for the term you paid for.** Keep auto-renew on, or diarise the
  expiry, so you don't lose it.
- **Some endings have rules.** Certain TLDs are limited to specific countries or
  uses; we'll tell you at checkout if one applies.

If a name you want shows as unavailable but unused, it may be registered elsewhere —
you can try to *transfer it to us* once you control it, or pick another ending.
