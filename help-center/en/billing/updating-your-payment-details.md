Keeping a valid payment method on file is the simplest way to make sure your services
never lapse. Here's how to add, change, and remove them, and what to do when a card
expires.

## Where to manage payment methods

Go to **Billing → Payment methods** (or similar) in your panel. From there you can
add a new method, set a default, and remove old ones.

## Add a payment method

1. Open **Billing → Payment methods**.
2. Choose **Add** and pick the type (card, PayPal, etc.).
3. Enter the details — for a card, make sure the **billing address matches** what
   your bank has on file.
4. Save. It's stored securely with our payment provider as a token, not as raw card
   data with us (see *Payment methods we accept*).

## Set your default

If you have more than one method, set the **default** — that's the one we use for
automatic renewals. Keep it current so renewals don't fail (see *Managing your
subscription*).

## When a card expires

An expired card is the most common cause of a failed renewal.

1. **Add the new card** before the old one expires.
2. **Set it as default.**
3. **Remove the old card** once the new one is confirmed.

We'll usually remind you as expiry approaches, but updating early avoids any gap.

## Remove a payment method

You can remove a method you no longer use. If it's the default, set another as
default first — otherwise automatic renewals will have nothing to charge and your
service could lapse (see *Failed payments and overdue services*).

## Fixing a declined payment

If a payment was declined:

- **Check the card details** — number, expiry, CVC.
- **Check the billing address** matches your bank's records — a mismatch is a common
  decline reason.
- **Check funds/limits** with your bank.
- **Try again**, or add a different method and retry the invoice.

## Security

- We **never store full card numbers**; details are held by a PCI-compliant provider.
- We will **never** ask for card details over email or chat — enter them only in the
  panel (see *Recognising phishing and scams*).
- Update payment details only while signed in to your own account.

## Troubleshooting

- **Can't add a card:** double-check every field, especially expiry and billing
  address; try a different browser if it won't submit.
- **Renewal failed despite a valid card:** confirm it's set as **default** and not
  expired; see *Failed payments and overdue services*.
- **Removed the wrong method:** just add it again; nothing is lost.
