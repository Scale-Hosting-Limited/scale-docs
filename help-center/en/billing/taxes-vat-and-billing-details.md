Tax and billing details are the boring-but-important side of invoices — get them
right and your accounts stay tidy. This explains how tax is applied and how to keep
your invoice details accurate.

## How tax appears

Where tax (such as VAT) applies, it's calculated at checkout and shown on your
invoice, based on:

- **Your location** — tax rules depend on where you are.
- **The product** — different services can be treated differently.
- **Your status** — business vs individual, and whether a valid tax/VAT number is on
  file.

The invoice shows the **net amount, the tax, and the total** so it's clear what
you're paying and why (see *Understanding your invoice*).

## Keep your billing details accurate

Your billing details appear on every invoice, so keep them correct:

1. Go to **Account → Profile** / **Billing details**.
2. Check your **name/company, address, and country** are right.
3. Save. Future invoices use the updated details.

Accurate details matter for tax, for your own bookkeeping, and to avoid card declines
from an address mismatch (see *Updating your payment details*). See also *Updating
your profile and contact details*.

## Adding a VAT / tax number

If you're a business with a valid VAT/tax registration number:

1. Add it in your **billing details**.
2. Depending on your location and ours, this may change how tax is applied (for
   example, reverse-charge rules within the EU).

Enter it exactly as registered — an invalid number won't be accepted for tax
purposes.

## Invoices for your accounts

You can **download** any invoice as a PDF for your records (see *Paying and viewing
invoices*). It includes your billing details and the tax breakdown, which is what
your accountant or expense system needs.

## Currency and pricing

Prices are shown in the store's listed currency. If you pay by card in another
currency, **your bank** does the conversion at their rate — the amount on your
statement may differ slightly from the invoice due to that and any bank fees.

## Need an invoice changed?

- **Details wrong on a past invoice:** update your billing details for future ones,
  and open a ticket if you need an existing invoice reissued.
- **Need extra info on invoices** (a company name, PO number, tax ID): add it to your
  billing details, or ask via a ticket.

## Troubleshooting

- **Tax looks wrong:** check your country and any VAT number are set correctly; tax
  follows those.
- **Charged tax as a business:** make sure your valid VAT/tax number is on file
  before ordering — it can't always be applied retroactively.
- **Statement amount differs from the invoice:** that's your bank's currency
  conversion/fees, not a billing error.
- **Anything unclear:** open a ticket and we'll explain the charge (see *Opening a
  support ticket*).
