Got a discount code or some account credit? Here's how each works, how to apply
them, and what to do if one doesn't.

## Promo codes

A promo code gives a discount at checkout — a percentage off, an amount off, or a
deal on a first term.

**To use one:**

1. Add your product to the **cart**.
2. Enter the code in the **promo/coupon** box.
3. Apply it — the discount shows in the total before you pay.

**Good to know:**

- Codes often have **conditions** — a minimum spend, specific products, a first-order
  only, or an **expiry date**.
- Usually **one code per order**; they don't always stack.
- Some apply only to the **first term** and renewals return to the normal price —
  check the terms so renewals don't surprise you.

## Account credit

Account credit is a balance on your account that's automatically put toward invoices.
It can come from:

- A **downgrade** or proration adjustment (see *Upgrading or downgrading a service*).
- A **refund** issued as credit rather than back to your card (see *Cancellations and
  refunds*).
- A **top-up** or promotion, where offered.

**How it's used:** when you pay an invoice, available credit is applied first, and
you pay any remainder with your normal method. You don't have to do anything special
— it's used automatically.

**Check your balance** under **Billing** in your panel.

## Which applies when

- **Promo code** → reduces the price of a **new order** at checkout.
- **Account credit** → reduces the amount due on **any invoice**, automatically.

They're separate: a promo code lowers the invoice total, then credit can cover part
or all of what's left.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Invalid code":** check spelling and case, that it hasn't **expired**, and that
  your order meets its conditions (right product, minimum spend, first order).
- **"Code already used":** many are single-use per customer.
- **Discount smaller than expected:** the code may apply to only part of the order or
  only the first term.
- **Credit not applied:** confirm the balance is on the **same account**; it applies
  at payment time, so check the invoice total after selecting pay.
- **Still stuck:** open a ticket with the code and the order details and we'll take a
  look (see *Opening a support ticket*).

## A note on fairness

Promo codes are offered on set terms. Please don't try to abuse or share codes
against their conditions — misuse can lead to an order being adjusted or cancelled.
If you're unsure whether a code applies to your situation, just ask before ordering.
