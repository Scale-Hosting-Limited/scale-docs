Everything you're billed for shows up as an invoice under **Invoices** in your
account. Here's how to read them and what the amounts mean.

## What an invoice shows

- **Line items** — each service the invoice covers and the period it pays for.
- **Subtotal, tax, and total** — tax is added where it applies to your location
  and is shown separately.
- **Due date** — when payment is needed to keep the service running.
- **Status** — unpaid, paid, or cancelled.

## Renewals

Services renew automatically so they don't go offline. Before each renewal you'll
see the upcoming invoice and the amount, and — where required — the renewal price
and date are shown to you before you're charged. You can add or change your payment
method any time under **Account → Payment methods**.

## Paying an invoice

Open the unpaid invoice and pay it with a saved method or a new one. If a service
was suspended for non-payment, paying the invoice reinstates it (usually
automatically within a few minutes).

## Credits and coupons

- **Account credit** is applied automatically to your next invoice before any card
  charge.
- **Coupons/promo codes** are entered at checkout and reflected in the total.

## Taxes and business details

- Tax is calculated from your account's country/region — keep your billing details
  accurate.
- If you're a business and need your VAT/company details on invoices, add them in
  your billing profile so they appear correctly.

## Downloading invoices

Each invoice can be viewed and downloaded (PDF) for your records — useful for
expenses and accounting.

## Troubleshooting

- **Charged but service still suspended:** give it a few minutes to reinstate; if
  it doesn't, open a billing ticket with the invoice number.
- **Payment keeps failing:** check the card details and that it's not expired or
  blocked by your bank; try another method.
- **Unexpected amount:** check whether it includes tax, a proration for an
  upgrade, or covers more than one service. If it still looks wrong, open a ticket
  with the invoice number and we'll explain the line items.
- **Want to stop auto-renewal:** see *Cancellations and refunds*.
