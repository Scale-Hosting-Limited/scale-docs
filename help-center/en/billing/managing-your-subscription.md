Everything about payments lives under **Billing** and **Services** in the panel.

## Invoices and payments

- See past and upcoming invoices under **Invoices**. Each shows what it covers and
  its due date.
- Add or update a payment method under **Account → Payment methods**.
- We accept the payment methods shown at checkout (card and others depending on
  your region).

## Renewals

Services renew automatically so they don't go offline. You'll see the renewal
amount and date before you're charged — auto-renewal and its price are shown
before payment.

## Upgrade or downgrade

Open the service and choose **Upgrade**. Changes are prorated, so you only pay the
difference for the rest of the current period.

## Cancel

Open the service, choose **Cancel**, and pick when it should stop — at the end of
the paid period (keep using it until then) or immediately. The panel shows any
refund due and what is non-refundable before you confirm.

## Need a hand?

For anything billing-related, open a ticket — include the invoice number if it's
about a specific charge.
