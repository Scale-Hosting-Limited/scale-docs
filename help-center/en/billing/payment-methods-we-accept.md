We support a range of payment methods so you can pay however suits you. This covers
what's available, how recurring payments work, and how your details are protected.

## Available methods

At checkout you'll typically see options such as:

- **Card** — credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, and others).
- **PayPal** — pay from your balance or linked funding source.
- **Other online methods** — depending on your region, additional options may appear
  at checkout.

The exact list is shown when you pay; if you need something specific, ask via a
ticket.

## One-off vs recurring

- **One-off payment** — you pay each invoice manually when it's due.
- **Recurring / automatic** — you store a method and we charge it automatically when
  services renew, so nothing lapses. This is the smoothest option; see *Managing your
  subscription* and *Updating your payment details*.

You choose which you prefer, and you can change it later.

## How your details are protected

Your payment security matters to us:

- We **don't store full card numbers** on our systems. Card payments are handled by
  established payment providers who are **PCI DSS** compliant.
- Stored methods are held as secure **tokens** with the provider, not as raw card
  data with us.
- Payments are made over **encrypted** connections.

In short, your card details live with the payment processor, not in our database.

## Currency

Prices are shown in the currency listed in the store. If you pay with a card in a
different currency, your bank handles the conversion at their rate (see *Taxes, VAT
and billing details* for pricing specifics).

## Keeping payments smooth

- **Keep a valid method on file** and update it before cards expire (see *Updating
  your payment details*).
- **Match your billing address** to your card to avoid declines.
- **Turn on auto-renew** so you don't miss a due date (see *Managing your
  subscription*).

## Troubleshooting

- **Card declined:** check the number, expiry, CVC, and that the billing address
  matches your card; then try again or use another method (see *Failed payments and
  overdue services*).
- **Payment taken but service not active:** allow a few minutes; some methods clear
  slowly. If it persists, open a ticket with the transaction reference.
- **Want a method we don't list:** ask via a ticket — we may be able to help.

## Security reminder

We will **never** ask you to send card details over email, chat, or Discord. Pay only
through the checkout in the panel. Anyone asking otherwise is not us (see *Recognising
phishing and scams*).
