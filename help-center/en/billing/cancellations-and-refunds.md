You're never locked in. This explains how to cancel cleanly and what to expect
around refunds.

## Cancelling a service

1. Open the service under **Services**.
2. Choose **Cancel**.
3. Pick **when** it should stop:
   - **End of the paid period** — you keep using it until the current term you've
     already paid for runs out, then it stops and won't renew. Best for most
     people.
   - **Immediately** — it stops now.
4. Confirm. The panel shows any refund due and what's non-refundable before you
   commit.

Cancelling stops future renewals — you won't be billed again for that service.

## Back up first

Cancelling eventually removes the service and its data. **Download anything you
want to keep before it ends** — your world, website files, databases, or mailbox
contents. See the relevant guides (e.g. *Managing worlds and backups*, *Managing
MySQL databases*). Once a service is fully terminated, its data can't be recovered.

## Refunds

Refund eligibility depends on the service, how long you've had it, and the terms
you agreed to at checkout — the **Terms of Service are authoritative**. In general:

- **New purchases** may qualify for a refund within a short initial window if the
  service hasn't been substantially used — this supports consumer cooling-off
  rights where they apply.
- **Renewals** and services already used through their term are generally not
  refundable.
- Amounts already consumed (a partly-used month, one-off setup fees, domain
  registrations) are typically non-refundable.

The cancellation screen shows the specific refund-due and non-refundable figures
for your service before you confirm, so you can decide with the numbers in front of
you.

## Domains are special

Domain registrations are paid to the registry and generally **can't be refunded**
once registered, even if you cancel related hosting. A domain you've paid for is
yours for the term regardless.

## Changed your mind?

If you cancelled at end-of-term and want to keep the service, you can usually undo
it before the term ends — reopen the service or contact us. If it's already
terminated, you'd order fresh.

## Need help?

For anything refund-related, open a **billing ticket** with the invoice or service
name. If you believe you're entitled to a refund the panel didn't offer, tell us
why and we'll review it against the terms and your consumer rights.
