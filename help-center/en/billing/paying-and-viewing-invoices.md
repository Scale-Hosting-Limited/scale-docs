Every charge on your account has an invoice — a record of what you're paying for and
when. This shows you how to find them, pay an outstanding one, and download copies
for your accounts.

## Find your invoices

Go to **Billing → Invoices** in your panel. You'll see a list with each invoice's:

- **Number and date**
- **Amount and status** — paid, unpaid, or overdue
- **What it's for** — the services on it

Click any invoice to see the full breakdown (see *Understanding your invoice* for
what each line means).

## Pay an outstanding invoice

1. Open **Billing → Invoices** and find the **unpaid** one.
2. Click it, then choose **Pay**.
3. Select a payment method (or add one — see *Updating your payment details*).
4. Confirm. The status changes to **paid** once payment clears, and any pending
   service activates.

If you have **auto-renew** and a valid default method, renewal invoices are paid
automatically — you don't need to do this manually (see *Managing your
subscription*).

## Download a copy

Need an invoice for your records or accounts? Open it and use **Download** (usually a
PDF). This is handy for expense claims and bookkeeping. Make sure your billing
details are correct first, since they appear on the invoice (see *Taxes, VAT and
billing details*).

## Invoice statuses

- **Unpaid** — issued and awaiting payment; pay before the due date.
- **Paid** — settled; nothing to do.
- **Overdue** — past its due date; pay promptly to avoid suspension (see *Failed
  payments and overdue services*).
- **Cancelled/Refunded** — no longer payable or has been refunded (see
  *Cancellations and refunds*).

## When invoices are generated

Renewal invoices are typically created **ahead of** the due date, giving you time to
pay before the service renews. Pay early — especially without auto-renew — so nothing
lapses.

## Troubleshooting

- **Paid but still shows unpaid:** some methods take time to clear; refresh after a
  few minutes. If it persists, open a ticket with the transaction reference.
- **Charged twice:** check whether one is pending vs settled; if it's a genuine
  duplicate, open a ticket and we'll sort a refund (see *Cancellations and refunds*).
- **Don't recognise an invoice:** open it to see which service renewed; if it's still
  unclear, contact us (see *Opening a support ticket*).
- **Wrong details on the invoice:** update your billing details, then future invoices
  will be correct; ask us if you need an existing one reissued.
