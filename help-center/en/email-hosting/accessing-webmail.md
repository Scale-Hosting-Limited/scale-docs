Webmail lets you use your mailbox from any browser — handy on a shared computer,
for a quick check, or to confirm a new mailbox works before you set up your phone.

## Sign in

1. Go to the webmail URL for your email hosting (shown on your service page —
   often `mail.yourdomain.com` or a `/webmail` link).
2. Enter your **full email address** as the username and your **mailbox password**
   (the one you set when creating the mailbox — not your Scale account password).
3. Sign in.

## What you can do

- Read, reply, and compose mail.
- Manage folders, and set up a **signature** and an **auto-responder / vacation
  reply** in settings.
- Create **filters** to sort incoming mail into folders.

## Webmail vs an email app

- **Webmail** is great for occasional access and quick checks — nothing to set up.
- An **email app** (Outlook, Apple Mail, your phone) is better for everyday use and
  works offline. See *Connecting your email client* for IMAP/SMTP settings.

Both use the same mailbox, so mail stays in sync (as long as your app uses IMAP,
not POP3).

## Troubleshooting

- **Wrong password:** you're likely using your Scale account password. Webmail
  wants the **mailbox** password. Reset it from **Mailboxes** in the panel if
  you're unsure.
- **Page won't load:** the mail domain may not be pointed yet — try the direct
  webmail link from your service page, and check your DNS/MX records (see
  *Creating a mailbox*).
- **Can read but can't send:** usually a temporary issue or your mailbox is over
  quota — free up space or check the service page for limits.
