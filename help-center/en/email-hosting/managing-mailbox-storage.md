Every mailbox has a storage limit. Hit it and new mail bounces — the classic
"mailbox full" problem. This shows you how to check usage, clear space, and keep your
mailboxes healthy.

## Why it matters

A full mailbox **can't receive new mail** — senders get a bounce and assume you
ignored them. Because email accumulates quietly (attachments especially), mailboxes
fill up over time without you noticing until it's too late.

## Check your usage

- In your email panel, each mailbox shows its **usage vs quota**.
- In **webmail**, you can usually see how full the mailbox is in the settings or
  folder view.

Check the mailboxes that receive the most, and any with big attachments.

## Free up space

1. **Empty Trash and Junk** — these count toward your quota and are often huge.
2. **Delete large attachments** — sort by size and clear the biggest first; save
   anything important elsewhere.
3. **Clear old Sent mail** — a frequently forgotten space hog.
4. **Archive** old mail you must keep by downloading it locally, then removing it from
   the server.

Emptying folders isn't always instant — some clients need the folder **expunged** or
the Trash truly emptied to reclaim the space.

## IMAP vs POP and storage

- **IMAP** keeps mail **on the server**, synced across devices — great, but it means
  your mail counts against the server quota. Managing space matters here.
- **POP** can **download and remove** mail from the server, freeing space — but then
  it only lives on that one device. Choose deliberately (see *Connecting your email
  client*).

## Increase your quota

If you regularly need more space:

- **Raise the mailbox quota** if your plan has headroom to allocate.
- **Upgrade your email plan** for more total storage (see *Upgrading or downgrading a
  service*).

Bigger quotas beat constantly clearing space if email is central to how you work.

## Prevent full mailboxes

- **Empty Trash/Junk regularly** — set your client to do it automatically.
- **Watch attachment-heavy mailboxes.**
- **Use aliases** feeding one well-managed mailbox rather than many neglected ones
  (see *Aliases, forwarders, and catch-all*).

## Troubleshooting

- **Senders getting "mailbox full":** clear space (Trash, Junk, big attachments) or
  raise the quota — do this promptly so you stop losing mail.
- **Deleted mail but space didn't free up:** empty Trash and expunge; some clients
  hold "deleted" mail until then.
- **Not sure which mailbox is full:** check usage per mailbox in the panel; the
  busiest one is usually the culprit. See *Email not sending or receiving* for related
  issues.
