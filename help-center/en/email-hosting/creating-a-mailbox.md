Email hosting lets you use addresses on your own domain, like
`you@yourdomain.com`.

## Point your domain's mail records

Before mail will flow, your domain needs the right DNS records. From your email
service page, copy the **MX**, **SPF**, and **DKIM** records shown and add them at
your domain registrar. These tell the world to deliver your mail to us and help it
avoid spam folders.

If your domain is with us, these are added for you.

## Create the mailbox

1. Open your email hosting service and go to **Mailboxes**.
2. Click **Create mailbox**, enter the address (the part before `@`) and set a
   strong password.
3. Choose the storage size for the mailbox if prompted.

That's it — the mailbox is ready to send and receive.
