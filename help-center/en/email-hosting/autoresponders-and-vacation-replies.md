An autoresponder sends an automatic reply to anyone who emails you — perfect for "I'm
away until Monday" or "thanks, we'll be in touch within a day." Here's how to set one
up well, without annoying your senders.

## When to use one

- **Out of office / holiday** — let people know you're away and when you'll reply.
- **Acknowledgement** — confirm a `support@` or `sales@` message was received and give
  a rough response time.
- **Address change** — tell senders to use a new address.

## Set up an autoresponder

1. In your email panel, open **Autoresponders** (sometimes under a mailbox's
   settings).
2. Choose the **mailbox** it applies to.
3. Set the **subject** and **message** of the automatic reply.
4. Set a **start and end date/time** if it's temporary (e.g. a holiday) — this is the
   key bit; it turns itself off so you don't leave it running.
5. Save.

## Writing a good auto-reply

Keep it short, clear, and useful:

- **Say you're away** and **when you'll be back** (or your normal response time).
- **Give an alternative** — another contact or address for anything urgent.
- **Don't overshare.** Avoid stating exact travel dates or that your home is empty —
  that's a security consideration.

Example: *"Thanks for your email. I'm away until Monday 5th and will reply when I'm
back. For anything urgent, contact sales@yourname.com."*

## Avoid reply loops and spam annoyance

- Good mail systems only auto-reply **once per sender** over a period — so you don't
  spam someone who emails twice. Ours follows that norm; you don't need to do
  anything.
- **Set an end date** so it stops automatically. A vacation reply left on for months
  looks unprofessional.
- Be aware that autoresponders reply to **everything**, including newsletters — the
  once-per-sender behaviour limits the noise.

## Turn it off

If you set an end date, it stops itself. To end early or edit it, go back to
**Autoresponders**, and disable or delete it.

## Troubleshooting

- **Not sending replies:** confirm it's enabled and within its start/end window, and
  the mailbox is receiving mail at all (see *Email not sending or receiving*).
- **Replying too often:** that's unusual — it should be once per sender per period;
  if it's looping, disable it and open a ticket.
- **Reply went to spam for the recipient:** make sure your SPF/DKIM are set (see
  *Setting up email DNS records*).
