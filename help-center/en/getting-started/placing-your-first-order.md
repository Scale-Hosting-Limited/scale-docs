Ready to get your first service? Ordering takes a few minutes. This walks you
through it end to end, so there are no surprises at checkout.

## Before you order

- **Have an account** and be signed in (see *Creating your account*).
- **Know roughly what you need** — the game or product, and a rough size. See
  *Choosing the right plan* if you're unsure; you can upgrade later.

## Step by step

1. **Pick your product** — game server, web hosting, email, Discord bot hosting, or
   a domain — from the store.
2. **Choose a plan** sized for your needs. Don't over-buy; most services can be
   upgraded once you know your real usage.
3. **Configure the options** shown — things like server location, game type, or
   billing cycle. Picking a location close to you or your players reduces latency.
4. **Choose a billing cycle** — monthly, quarterly, or longer. Longer cycles often
   cost less per month.
5. **Add to cart**, then review everything before paying.

## Checkout

1. Review the items and total in your **cart**.
2. Enter or confirm your **billing details** — accurate details matter for invoices
   and, for domains, for registration (see *Updating your profile and contact
   details*).
3. Apply a **promo code** if you have one (see *Account credit and promo codes*).
4. Choose a **payment method** and pay (see *Payment methods we accept*).

## After you pay

Most services are **provisioned automatically** within a few minutes. You'll get a
confirmation email, and the service appears under **Services** in your dashboard.
For what to expect next, see *What happens after you order*.

## Setting up

Once it's ready, open the service and follow its getting-started guide:

- Game servers → *Creating and managing a game server* / *Setting up your Minecraft
  server*
- Web hosting → *Getting started with web hosting*
- Email → *Creating a mailbox*
- Discord bot → *Deploying your first bot*

## Troubleshooting

- **Service not appearing:** give it a few minutes; if it's still missing, check for
  a pending invoice (some products activate after payment clears) and see *Failed
  payments and overdue services*.
- **Paid but no confirmation:** check spam, confirm your account email, then open a
  ticket if needed.
- **Chose the wrong plan:** you can usually upgrade or change it — see *Upgrading or
  downgrading a service*.
