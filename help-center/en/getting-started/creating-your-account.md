Welcome to Scale Hosting. Creating an account takes a minute.

## Register

1. Go to the sign-up page and enter your email, a password, and your date of
   birth. We ask for your date of birth to confirm you are old enough to use the
   service — you must be at least 13.
2. Accept the terms of service and complete the sign-up.
3. Check your inbox for a verification email and click the link to confirm your
   address. You can follow the link even if you are not signed in.

## Secure it straight away

Before you order anything, turn on two-factor authentication from
**Account → Security**. It is the single best protection for your services and
billing. See *Enabling two-factor authentication* in the Account & Security
section.
