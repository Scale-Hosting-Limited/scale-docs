Welcome aboard! Whether you're here to run a Minecraft server for friends, host a
website, set up email on your own domain, or keep a Discord bot online, you're in
the right place. This is the two-minute overview to get your bearings.

## What we host

- **Game servers** — Minecraft and many other games, managed from an easy panel.
  See the *Minecraft* and *Game Servers* sections.
- **Web hosting** — websites, WordPress, databases, and SSL. See *Web Hosting*.
- **Email hosting** — mailboxes on your own domain. See *Email Hosting*.
- **Discord bot hosting** — run your own bot 24/7. See *Discord Bot Hosting*.
- **Domains** — register or transfer domain names and manage DNS. See *Domains*.

Everything lives under **one account and one panel**, so your services, domains, and
billing are all in one place.

## Getting started in three steps

1. **Create your account** and secure it — see *Creating your account* and turn on
   two-factor authentication (see *Enabling two-factor authentication*).
2. **Place your first order** for the service you want — see *Placing your first
   order*.
3. **Set it up** using the guide for your product — each section has a
   "getting started" article to walk you through it.

## Finding your way around

Your home base is the **dashboard**, where you'll see your services, invoices, and
account settings. New to it? See *Navigating your dashboard*.

## Getting help

- **This Help Center** covers the common questions — browse by section or search.
- **Support tickets** reach our team for anything specific to your account — see
  *Opening a support ticket*.
- **Status and maintenance** notices tell you if something's affecting our
  platform.

## A few tips for a smooth start

- **Secure your account first** — 2FA takes two minutes and protects everything
  else.
- **Keep your contact details current** so invoices and important notices reach you.
- **Read the getting-started guide** for your product before diving in — it saves
  time.

Ready? Head to *Creating your account* if you haven't signed up, or *Placing your
first order* if you're set to go.
