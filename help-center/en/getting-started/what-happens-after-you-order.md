You've paid — now what? Most of the time your service is ready almost immediately.
Here's exactly what happens behind the scenes and how to find and start using your
new service.

## Provisioning

**Provisioning** is us setting up your service automatically. For most products —
game servers, web hosting, email, bot hosting — this happens **within a few
minutes** of payment clearing. You don't need to do anything; the panel builds it
for you.

A few things can take a little longer:

- **Domains** register in minutes but may need an **email verification** step (watch
  your inbox).
- **Orders paid by a method that clears slowly** activate once payment confirms.
- **Manual review** occasionally applies to a first order for fraud checks — rare,
  and we'll be in touch if so.

## Your confirmation

You'll get a **confirmation email** with the order details, and the service appears
under **Services** in your dashboard. If you set login or connection details during
setup, they'll be there too.

## Finding your service

1. Sign in and open your **dashboard**.
2. Go to **Services** — your new service is listed, with its status.
3. Click it to open its management panel (the game panel, web hosting control panel,
   and so on).

See *Navigating your dashboard* for a fuller tour.

## First steps by product

- **Game server** → open the game panel, hit **Start**, watch the console come
  online (see *Creating and managing a game server*).
- **Web hosting** → upload your site and point your domain (see *Getting started
  with web hosting*).
- **Email** → create your first mailbox and set DNS (see *Creating a mailbox* and
  *Setting up email DNS records*).
- **Discord bot** → deploy your code and add your token (see *Deploying your first
  bot*).
- **Domain** → verify the registrant email, then point it where you need (see
  *Pointing your domain*).

## Your first invoice and renewals

Your order created an invoice, now marked paid (see *Understanding your invoice*).
The service will **renew** on its cycle — keep a valid payment method on file and
consider auto-renew so nothing lapses (see *Managing your subscription*).

## If something's not right

- **Service still "pending" after a while:** check for an unpaid invoice; see
  *Failed payments and overdue services*.
- **Can't find the service:** make sure you're signed in to the account you ordered
  with.
- **Anything unclear:** open a ticket with your order number (see *Opening a support
  ticket*).
