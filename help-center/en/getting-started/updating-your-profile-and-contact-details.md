Your profile details do more than fill in a form — they appear on invoices, they're
used to register domains, and they're how we reach you about your services. Keeping
them accurate avoids billing and domain problems down the line.

## Where to update them

Go to **Account → Profile** (or **Settings**). From there you can edit your name,
company (if any), address, and phone number. Save when you're done.

To change the **email address** you sign in with and receive notices at, see
*Changing your account email* — that has its own secure process.

## Why accuracy matters

- **Invoices** — your billing details appear on every invoice; wrong details cause
  accounting and tax headaches (see *Taxes, VAT and billing details*).
- **Domains** — registrant details come from your profile. **False or invalid
  domain contact info can get a domain suspended** under ICANN rules (see *Domain
  privacy and WHOIS*).
- **Reaching you** — renewal reminders, security alerts, and service notices go to
  your contact details.

## Keep your phone and address current

If you move or change number, update it promptly. It matters most for:

- **Ownership verification** if you're ever recovering an account.
- **Domain registrant records**, which must be kept accurate.
- **Payment methods**, where a billing address must match your card.

## Notification preferences

Under **Account → Notifications** you can choose which **non-essential** messages you
receive — product news and general notices. **Security and billing notices are
always sent**, so you never miss a sign-in alert or an invoice (see *Security alerts
and notifications*).

## Your data and privacy

You can view and correct the personal data we hold from your profile at any time.
For the full picture of what we store and your rights to access, correct, or delete
it, see *Your data and privacy rights*.

## Troubleshooting

- **Can't save a change:** check required fields are filled and the format is valid
  (especially phone and postcode).
- **Card declined after moving:** update your billing address to match your card
  (see *Updating your payment details*).
- **Domain warning email:** it may be an ICANN request to verify/correct registrant
  details — action it promptly to avoid suspension.
