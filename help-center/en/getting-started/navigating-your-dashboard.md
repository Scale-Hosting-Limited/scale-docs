Once you're signed in, everything you need is a click or two away. Here's the lay
of the land.

## The main areas

**Dashboard** — your home page: a summary of active services, anything that needs
attention (an unpaid invoice, a notice), and quick links.

**Services** — every product you have with us: game servers, web hosting, email,
domains. Click a service to open its management panel — the game panel for a
server, the control panel for web/email, and so on. This is where you'll spend
most of your time.

**Invoices** — your billing history and anything unpaid. Open an invoice to pay it
or download a PDF (see *Understanding your invoice*).

**Support / Tickets** — open a ticket and track replies (see *Opening a support
ticket*).

**Account** — your profile, security settings, payment methods, and notification
preferences.

## Managing a service

Open a service from **Services** to reach its controls. Depending on the product
you'll see things like:

- **Game servers:** console, file manager, backups, startup/version settings,
  subdomains, and content (plugins/mods/modpacks).
- **Web hosting:** file manager, databases, email routing, SSL, and domains.
- **Email:** mailboxes and webmail.

Actions like start/stop/restart, upgrade, and cancel live on the service page too.

## Your account settings

Under **Account** you can:

- **Security** — set up 2FA/passkeys, change your password (see *Enabling
  two-factor authentication*).
- **Payment methods** — add or update how you pay.
- **Notifications** — choose what we email you about.
- **Profile / billing details** — keep your name, email, and billing info accurate
  (it affects invoices and tax).

## Tips

- The item that **needs attention** (unpaid invoice, action required) is surfaced
  on the dashboard first — check there if something isn't working.
- Bookmark the panel and keep 2FA on — your account controls all your services and
  billing.
- Not sure where something is? Open a ticket and we'll point you straight to it.
