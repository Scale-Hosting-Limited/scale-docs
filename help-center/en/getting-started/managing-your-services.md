Everything you host with us lives under **Services** in your dashboard. This is
where you open each one, check its status, change its plan, or cancel it. Here's how
to find your way around.

## The Services list

Go to **Services** to see everything on your account. Each entry shows:

- **What it is** — the product and plan.
- **Status** — active, suspended, pending, or cancelled.
- **Next due date** — when it renews and the amount.

Click any service to open its **management panel** — the game panel for a game
server, the control panel for web hosting, and so on.

## Understanding status

- **Active** — running normally.
- **Pending** — ordered but not yet provisioned, or awaiting payment (see *What
  happens after you order*).
- **Suspended** — paused, usually for an unpaid invoice or a policy issue (see
  *Failed payments and overdue services* and *Understanding suspensions and
  appeals*).
- **Cancelled/Terminated** — ended; data may be removed after a grace period.

## Upgrading or changing a plan

Outgrown your plan, or over-bought? Most services can change tier.

1. Open the service (or find the **Upgrade/Downgrade** option next to it).
2. Choose the new plan; the panel shows any price difference.
3. Confirm and pay any difference. See *Upgrading or downgrading a service* for how
   proration works.

## Renewals

Services renew on their billing cycle. To avoid interruptions:

- Keep a **valid payment method** on file (see *Updating your payment details*).
- Turn on **auto-renew** where offered.
- Watch for **renewal invoices** and pay them before the due date.

See *Managing your subscription* for cycles and auto-renew.

## Cancelling a service

If you no longer need something, you can request cancellation — either at the **end
of the current period** (keep using it until then) or **immediately**. Back up
anything you want to keep first, as data is removed after cancellation. See
*Cancellations and refunds*.

## Keeping track

- **Label things clearly** where the panel lets you nickname a service.
- **Review your Services list** occasionally so you're not paying for something
  you've stopped using.
- **Check due dates** so renewals never catch you out.

## Troubleshooting

- **Service missing:** confirm you're on the right account; cancelled services may
  drop off the list.
- **Can't open the panel:** if it's suspended, resolve the cause (often an invoice)
  first.
- **Unexpected charge:** open the invoice to see what renewed; see *Understanding
  your invoice*.
