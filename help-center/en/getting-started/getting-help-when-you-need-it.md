Stuck on something? You've got options, and picking the right one gets you an answer
faster. Here's how to get help — and how to help us help you.

## Try these first

Most questions have a quick answer:

1. **Search this Help Center.** It covers setup, billing, security, and
   troubleshooting for every product. Browse by section or use search.
2. **Check the troubleshooting guides** for common problems — *Server won't start*,
   *Can't connect to your server*, *Website not loading*, and more.
3. **Check status/maintenance notices** in the panel to see if a known issue is
   affecting the platform.

Self-service is often the fastest route — many issues are solved in minutes with the
right guide.

## When to open a ticket

For anything specific to **your** account — billing questions, a service that's
misbehaving, verification, or something the guides don't cover — open a support
ticket. See *Opening a support ticket* for how, and *Choosing the right support
channel* to pick the best route.

## Help us help you

A good ticket gets a faster, better answer. Include:

- **Which service** it's about (name/ID) and your **order/invoice number** if
  relevant.
- **What you expected** and **what actually happened**.
- **Exact error messages** — copy them from the console or screen (see *Reading
  server logs*).
- **What you've already tried**, so we don't repeat it.

"My server won't start, here's the console error" gets solved far faster than "it's
broken."

## What to expect

- We aim to respond within our stated **response times**, prioritising urgent issues
  like outages over general questions (see *Our response times and priorities*).
- We may ask **ownership questions** for account-sensitive requests — that's to
  protect you (see *Recovering a locked account*).
- If an issue needs escalating, we'll do that and keep you updated (see *Escalating
  and following up*).

## Staying safe while getting help

- **We'll never ask for your password or 2FA codes.** Anyone who does is not us (see
  *Recognising phishing and scams*).
- Only get support through **official channels** — the panel and our verified
  presences — not random DMs.
